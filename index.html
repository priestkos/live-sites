Free LLM APIs

Every LLM API with a free tier that stays free

A directory of LLM APIs with permanent free tiers for text inference. Every provider is a collapsible card, each base URL copies in one click, and every link goes straight to that provider's API key page. All endpoints are OpenAI SDK-compatible unless a card says otherwise.

The content is mirrored verbatim from mnfst/awesome-free-llm-apis (CC0-1.0); a scheduled workflow refreshes it once a week and Pages serves it straight from main.

K'os Mos

The Crimson Kitsune, a bard and mercenary

The character page for K'os Mos in Final Fantasy XIV: courtesan, mercenary and bard, whose melodies carry as easily through chaotic frontlines as through dim, silk-draped parlours. She plays on Balmung and Tonberry, and the page says how to find her in game.

It is the oldest site here and the apex of kosmos14.date, the domain the three FFXIV references and the model hub live under. A daily build keeps it current, and visitors confirm they are 18 or older on the way in.

Free Models Hub

Four catalogs of free models, zero cost

One hub for four free-model catalogs: OpenRouter, OpenCode, Nous Portal and Command Code. Every model listed is priced at $0, the catalogs are refreshed daily, and the stealth models being served right now get a page of their own.

Each catalog is its own static page under the one subdomain, deployed by a GitHub Actions workflow.

Bard, Dancer and Scholar for Dawntrail

Both are single offline HTML files with no external assets, written against patch 7.5, and Pages serves them straight from main.
